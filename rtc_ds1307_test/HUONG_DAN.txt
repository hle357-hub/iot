CHUONG TRINH CHAN DOAN DS1307 RIENG

1. Mo rtc_ds1307_test.ino trong chinh thu muc rtc_ds1307_test.
2. Ngat USB tren web va dong Serial Monitor cua sketch khac.
3. Chon Arduino Nano va dung cong COM, roi Upload.
4. Mo Serial Monitor: 9600 baud, Newline hoac Both NL & CR.

No tu scan I2C khi khoi dong va doc RTC moi 5 giay.
Gui I2C de scan lai, RTC de doc ngay, HELP de xem lenh.
Gui ngay gio thuc te theo YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS neu muon dat dong ho.
Chuong trinh KHONG tu dat gio va KHONG ghi EEPROM.

DS1307: VCC -> 5V, GND -> GND, SDA -> A4, SCL -> A5 tren Nano.
Pin cuc ao la nguon du phong, khong thay cho VCC cua giao tiep I2C.
0x68 la dia chi RTC; 0x27/0x3F thuong la LCD.

Day la sketch test, KHONG chay do muc nuoc, den/coi hay gui SMS.
Test xong can nap lai D:/iot/sketch_oct30a/sketch_oct30a.ino de chay he thong chinh.

Da bien dich cho arduino:avr:nano: flash 8562 byte (27%), RAM 487 byte (23%).
Chua nap va kiem tra tren module DS1307 thuc te.
